CanonMerge is a collaborative fiction dApp for games, RPGs, and shared world platforms. A sponsor locks exactly 2 GEN and names two writers, who submit immutable sibling scenes against one onchain parent. GenLayer validators judge whether both changes can coexist under the locked canon and rules. Deterministic contract code checks exact world, parent, branch, attempt, coverage, and conflict fields before it creates one shared node with two continuation rights and 1 GEN credit each, or two forks with a 2 GEN sponsor refund. Unverifiable reviews move no rights or GEN. The public repo proves one pinned contract, 16 direct checks, real-SDK wallet preflight, CI, a finalized Studio Dev merge lifecycle, both withdrawals, zero remaining liability, and live canonical browser reads. V1 judges fictional consistency only; it does not prove copyright, real events, or offchain publication.
